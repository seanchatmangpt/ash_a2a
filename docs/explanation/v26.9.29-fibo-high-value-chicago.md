# v26.9.29 Chicago — FIBO High-Value Finance 80/20 Court

## Purpose

This court qualifies the minimum SA2A invariants required before a later
GraphLaw + FIBO + Knowledge Hooks + Reactor transaction simulation can be
treated as meaningful evidence.

It intentionally does **not** connect to a live payment rail and it does not
claim regulatory, SWIFT, Fedwire, securities-settlement, or legal compliance.

The v26.9.29 subject is narrower:

- FIBO-shaped transaction identity is canonical.
- Meaningful transaction drift changes the exact prepared subject.
- USD financial consequences use integer micro-units, never floating point.
- The USD 10M policy boundary is exact.
- A USD 25M consequence cannot pass a USD 10M financial envelope.
- Financial budget never manufactures authority.
- Semantic evidence is identity-bearing and authority-free.

## Canonical semantic shape

The fixture uses vocabulary already vendored in GraphLaw's FIBO estate:

- FND TransactionsExt / MarketTransactions / MarketTransaction
- FND Accounting / CurrencyAmount / MonetaryAmount
- ISO 4217 USD identity
- principal and counterparty identity
- payment terms
- settlement identity
- master-agreement identity

The test data is synthetic. No real counterparty, account, payment, security,
or external rail is touched.

## USD threshold

SA2A's current financial spend dimension is integer `money_micros`.

Therefore:

- USD 10,000,000 = 10,000,000,000,000 micros
- USD 25,000,000 = 25,000,000,000,000 micros

The court contains no floating-point conversion path.

The USD 10M threshold is a v26.9.29 Chicago policy fixture, not a claim that
FIBO itself defines a USD 10M authority threshold.

## Falsifiers

The court mutates each of these independently:

1. counterparty
2. amount
3. currency
4. master agreement
5. settlement identity
6. semantic graph digest
7. financial envelope
8. semantic evidence authority

A mutation must either change the prepared identity or be refused at the
appropriate boundary.

## Standing ceiling

PASS means the tested SA2A identity/evidence/budget invariants hold for this
synthetic high-value financial subject.

PASS does not mean:

- the transaction was legally valid;
- the transaction settled;
- a bank or payment network accepted it;
- FIBO coverage is exhaustive;
- a USD 10M threshold is universal;
- authority was inferred from transaction semantics.

Those require later courts with real GraphLaw reasoning, FIBO ontology
closure, Knowledge Hooks, Reactor/CommandBus execution, Affidavit standing,
independent settlement observation, replay/crash recovery, and rail-specific
adapters.
